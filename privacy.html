Privacy Policy — rclone-backups

rclone-backups is a personal, internal application owned by flixnettv. It is not distributed to the public.

What it does

It reads and writes files in the Google Drive of the authorizing account (the same user who grants access) to store encrypted personal backups.

Data handling

  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • All backups are stored only in the authorizing user's own Google Drive.
